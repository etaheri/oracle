Common questions

Why can't I answer yesterday's round?

Each question has its own deadline and may close before the round ends. A locked round is what makes the score mean anything — if answers could arrive after the world did, nothing would be a prediction.

Why is the lowest confidence 55%?

Because 50% is not a belief. If you genuinely have no view, the honest move is to skip the question rather than flip a coin for points.

A question got voided. What happened?

A question voids when its answer cannot be verified against its stated source, or when two independent readings of the outcome disagree. A disagreement always resolves to unverifiable — never to a winner. Voided questions score nothing for anyone, including the Oracle.

My streak broke and I thought I had a shield.

Shields are spent automatically at settlement on an eligible missed round when your streak is at least three days and a shield is available. One free shield is available each calendar month, then paid reserves are used. A shield preserves the count without adding a played day; an active subscription alone does not guarantee protection. If a streak broke anyway, write in with the date and it can be checked against the record.

How do I move my record to a new phone?

Sign in with Apple. That binds the record to your Apple account so it can be restored. Without it, the record lives only on the device that made it.

How do I delete my data?

Clear your identity in the app to detach the device, or write in to have the record itself deleted. See the privacy policy for what is stored in the first place.

Subscriptions

Outsee Plus is billed through Apple. Manage or cancel it in Settings → your name → Subscriptions on your device; cancelling there takes effect at the end of the current period. Refunds are handled by Apple, not by us — request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.
