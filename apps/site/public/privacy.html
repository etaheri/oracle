Privacy Policy

Outsee is a game about predictions, not about you. It has no account name, no email address, and no profile. This page describes exactly what it does store.

What Outsee collects

DataWhy
A random identifierCreated on your device the first time you open the app, so your answers and streak persist. It is not derived from any hardware identifier and is not shared with other apps.
Your predictionsEach answer and its confidence, plus the score and streak that follow. This is the game.
PlatformThat the device is iOS. Used to route notifications correctly.
A hashed IPWhen the app first registers, the IP address is combined with a secret and one-way hashed. Only that hash is stored, and only to limit how many accounts can be created from one place. The IP address itself is never written down.
Sign in with Apple (optional)If you choose to sign in so your record survives a new phone, Apple gives us an opaque identifier for you. We do not receive or store your name or email address.
Purchase statusWhether a subscription is active and how many shields remain. Payment is handled entirely by Apple — we never see card details.
Notification token (optional)Only if you allow notifications.
Crash and usage diagnosticsWhich screens are reached and what errors occur, so the app can be fixed.

What Outsee does not collect

  • No name, email address, phone number, or contact list.
  • No location. The app contains no location code and requests no location permission.
  • No tracking. Nothing you do here is linked to your activity in other companies' apps or websites. There is no advertising identifier and no ad network, which is why the app never shows a tracking prompt.
  • Motion stays on the device. The orb responds to how you hold your phone. Those sensor readings are used to draw the screen and are never transmitted or stored.

Who else processes it

OUTSEEN uses a small number of service providers, each for a single job. They process this data on our behalf, under contract, and are not permitted to use it for their own purposes.

ProviderWhat it handles
ApplePayments, Sign in with Apple, and delivery of notifications. We never see card details.
CloudflareRuns the app's server and this website.
NeonThe database holding your record and your predictions.
RevenueCatKeeps track of whether a subscription is active.
OneSignalSends notifications, if you have allowed them.
PostHogProduct analytics — which screens are reached.
SentryCrash and error reports.
AnthropicWrites and checks the daily questions. See below.

Your predictions and identifiers are never sold, and never shared for advertising.

Questions are written and resolved with the help of a language model. Only the question text and its public sources are sent for that — no player data of any kind leaves for this purpose. Your answers are never used to train anyone's model.

How long it is kept

Your record is kept while you use the app, because a streak and a score are cumulative by nature. Published rounds and their outcomes remain part of the game's public history.

Deleting your data

You can clear your identity from within the app, which detaches the device from its record. To have the record itself deleted, contact us at the address on the support page and it will be removed.

Children

Outsee is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If this policy changes materially, the effective date above changes with it.
