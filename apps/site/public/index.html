A daily prediction game

Make your call. Outsee the Oracle. Outscore the field.

Five questions about what happens next. Make your calls, choose how sure you are, then find out whether you outscored the Oracle and other players.

How to play

  • Five real-world questions each daily round. Choose yes or no and confidence from 55% to 95%. Each question has its own deadline.
  • Seal your call. Your answer and confidence lock when submitted. The crowd shows aggregate predictions after you commit; the Oracle makes predictions using AI. Its forecasts stay hidden until reveal.
  • Face the result. Confidence pays more when right and costs more when wrong. Results arrive after verification, which can take longer than the question deadline. Unresolved outcomes are pending, never losses.
  • Find your place on the daily board. The daily board ranks you among eligible players, with the Oracle shown for comparison. Answer every non-void question; at least three must resolve. A ranking requires enough eligible players.
  • Build your record. Your ledger keeps your predictions and results. Your forecast rating starts after 50 cumulative qualifying calls, not consecutive days. Confidence history shows how your beliefs matched outcomes.

Try a call before the next round

Arrived after the deadline? Answer any questions still open, or try an unranked exhibition with an immediate result. Exhibitions use a past round or a clearly labeled fictional example. Your live record stays untouched.

Keep a vigil

Your vigil is your playing streak: a reason to make one call each day. Seal one daily call to keep it going; the count updates when the round settles. It adds no points. Your rating comes from your forecasts. After a gap, your predictions, results and rating remain.

Play for free

Daily play and competitive opportunities are free. Outsee Plus adds optional shields: they protect an eligible streak of at least three days through a missed round, without adding calls, wins or points. Protection requires an available shield. One free shield is available per calendar month; Plus grants three paid shields per billing period, capped at five paid shields at each grant.
