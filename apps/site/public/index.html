Five questions. One a day.

Can you outsee the machine?

Every day at noon, five questions about what happens next. You answer each one and say how sure you are. The next day the world answers, and you find out who was right — you, the crowd, or the Oracle.

How it works

  • Five questions, once a day. They open at noon Eastern and lock 24 hours later.
  • Say how sure you are. Confidence runs from 55% to 95%. There are no coin flips — 55 is the least belief you can hold.
  • Being confident and wrong costs more than being unsure and wrong. Calibration is the whole game.
  • The Oracle plays too. It commits its own forecast before the round opens and before it can see a single human answer, so it can never copy the crowd.
  • Nothing is revised. Once a round opens, its questions cannot be edited — that rule is enforced by the database itself, not by good intentions.

What it costs

The daily round is free and always will be. An optional subscription adds shields, which protect a streak on a day you miss.

Not yet on the App Store. OUTSEEN is in TestFlight while its first rounds run. This site exists so the app has a real privacy policy and a real place to ask for help.
