# Two-session gameplay playtest

Recruit five newcomers; use anonymous participant labels P1–P5. Do not send invitations automatically. Use local notes if analytics is unconfigured.

## Session one

1. Hand over the opening screen without explaining the game. Ask: “What do you think you will do here?”
2. Ask them to try practice, choose a side, change confidence, cancel, and seal. Observe before helping. Record unintended commitments.
3. Let them play the real round. Time the first successful live seal and the full round. Ask what their confidence means and what being wrong would cost.
4. Ask which question they most want answered and why. Record unfamiliar vocabulary, source confusion, and abandoned questions.
5. Ask what they expect to happen next and when they would return.

## Session two

Bring the same participants back after real outcomes exist. Before explanation, ask who won, why, which call mattered, whether the evidence convinced them, and what they want to do next. Record result interpretation and recalled questions.

Separately walk through low crowd, missing forecast, pending results, an early global void, a majority-void day, and a missed day. These walkthroughs do not count as genuine return behavior. Ask whether preserving a streak remains desirable when it no longer changes competitive points. Do not change pricing based on one interview.

## Acceptance notes

Formative targets: at least 4/5 can explain confidence, avoid an unintended first live commitment, recall a question, and interpret the result correctly. Report actual counts; five interviews cannot establish retention uplift or willingness to pay.

Record: participant, first-seal time, round time, practice cancel success, accidental seal count, completion, recalled question, duel interpretation, evidence trust, void confusion, return intent, and smallest proposed correction. Keep free-text interview notes out of analytics.

## Native checklist

Check first install and returning navigation; practice pull, center cancellation, interrupted gesture, hold buttons, VoiceOver, large text and reduced motion; live confidence/payoff and Big One weight; local deadline boundaries; reveal win/loss/tie/pending/missing forecast/void; source links; and the native share sheet. Practice must never write predictions or game progress. Record device and OS with each result.
